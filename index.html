MESBG Fight Calculator

Odds for one fight in the Middle-earth Strategy Battle Game (2024 rules). Change any number and the results update.

Rules this calculator uses
  • Each side rolls one die per Attack. Highest die wins. On a tie, higher Fight wins. If Fight is also tied, roll off 50/50.
  • Elf weapon: wins the roll-off on 1–4 instead of 1–3. Does nothing if both sides have one. A model that is shielding loses this.
  • Two-handed weapon: −1 to each duel die unless it's a natural 6. +1 to wound, on both rolls when two are needed (6 then 4 becomes 5 then 3).
  • Shielding: the model rolls twice its Attacks in the duel but makes no strikes if its side wins. Not allowed while a spear or pike supports the side.
  • Spear or pike support: adds 1 die to the duel and 1 strike. Supporters can't be hit (the Monster exception isn't included yet).
  • Banner: each side can re-roll one duel die once, and only does it when that improves its chance to win. With 2 or more dice it re-rolls a die that isn't its best, which can't hurt. With 1 die on a tie that goes to a roll-off, it weighs re-rolling against the roll-off (tied on 1–3: re-roll; on 4–6: keep and roll off; an elf weapon shifts this). If both sides could use a banner, a 50/50 coin decides who chooses first.
  • Trapped: if a trapped side loses, the winners strike twice as many times.
  • The results show each enemy model in front separately, as if all strikes went at that one. Spear and pike supporters can't be hit. A roll of 1 always fails to wound.
  • Fate: each wound can be saved on a 4+, one roll per Fate point, until the model runs out.
  • Might is not included yet. All numbers are exact odds, worked out from every possible roll.
